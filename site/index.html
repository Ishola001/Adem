FAQ

Common questions

How long before results show?

SEO and content typically take 2–3 months to show meaningfully; email and paid ads can show movement within the first few weeks.

Do you work with brands outside ecommerce?

The core focus is ecommerce and DTC, but the same systems apply to most product-led brands — ask directly.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes — each service is scoped and priced on its own, nothing is bundled by default.

Is training offered alongside client work?

Yes — see the Training page for the current curriculum and how to apply.

Do you work with brands outside Nigeria?

Yes — engagements run fully remote, and current clients span the US, Europe, and beyond.

What platforms do you work on?

Shopify is the core focus for store management; email runs on Klaviyo, and ads run on Meta and Google.

How is pricing structured?

Each service is scoped individually after an audit call, based on the size of the store and the work involved — not a flat rate card.

Do you sign contracts or work month-to-month?

Both are available. Most engagements start month-to-month and move to a longer retainer once the systems are proven.
