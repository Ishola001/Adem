The email flows every ecommerce brand needs

Most stores send email campaigns but skip the flows — the automated sequences that trigger based on what a customer actually does. Flows run in the background and typically outperform one-off campaigns on a per-email basis, because they arrive at the exact moment someone is ready to act.

Welcome series

Sent the moment someone joins your list. This is where you set expectations, introduce the brand, and often make the first offer.

Abandoned cart

Triggered when a shopper adds a product but leaves without buying. A short, well-timed sequence recovers a meaningful share of that lost revenue.

Post-purchase

Confirms the order, sets delivery expectations, and opens the door to a second purchase down the line.

Win-back

Re-engages customers who haven't purchased in a while, before they forget your brand entirely.

Each of these should be built once, tested, and left running — that's what separates a brand earning steady revenue from email versus one treating it as an afterthought.

How retargeting works — and why it converts so well

Retargeting shows ads to people who've already interacted with your brand — visited your site, viewed a product, or added to cart without buying. It converts at a higher rate than cold prospecting because you're not introducing your brand, you're reminding someone who was already close to buying.

Why it works

  • The audience already knows your brand — no cold introduction needed
  • Intent is already established — a cart visit or product view is a strong signal
  • Frequency builds familiarity — repeated, relevant exposure nudges hesitant buyers

Where it fits in your budget

Retargeting should never compete with prospecting for the same budget — it's a separate, smaller pool spent on your warmest audience, and it typically returns the strongest numbers in the account.
